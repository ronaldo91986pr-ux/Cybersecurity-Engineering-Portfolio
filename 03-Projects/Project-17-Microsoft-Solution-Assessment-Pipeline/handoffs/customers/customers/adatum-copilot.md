# A. Datum Corporation — Copilot Readiness Opportunity

> Fictional customer scenario created for portfolio and training purposes.

## Opportunity Overview

**Customer:** A. Datum Corporation  
**Microsoft AE:** Michael Torres  
**Solution Area:** Copilot & AI  
**Assessment:** Copilot Readiness Assessment  
**Current Stage:** Qualification  
**Priority:** Medium

## Business Situation

A. Datum Corporation has approximately 1,200 employees and uses Microsoft 365 across multiple business departments.

Leadership is interested in Microsoft 365 Copilot to improve employee productivity, but the organization is unsure whether its current environment, data governance, security controls, and users are ready for broader Copilot adoption.

## Business Goals

- Improve employee productivity
- Reduce time spent on repetitive tasks
- Improve access to organizational knowledge
- Identify practical Copilot use cases
- Evaluate organizational readiness before wider adoption

## Customer Concerns

- Data security
- Oversharing of organizational information
- User permissions
- Copilot adoption
- Employee training
- Governance
- Demonstrating business value

## Discovery Questions

### Business

1. What business problems are you hoping Copilot will help solve?
2. Which departments or user groups are being considered first?
3. What would a successful Copilot implementation look like?

### Microsoft 365 Environment

4. Which Microsoft 365 services are currently used?
5. How are users, groups, and permissions currently managed?
6. Are there existing data governance or information protection policies?

### Security and Data

7. Are permissions regularly reviewed?
8. How is sensitive organizational information protected?
9. Are there concerns about users having access to information they should not see?

### Adoption

10. Which Copilot use cases would provide the greatest business value?
11. How will employees be trained to use Copilot effectively?
12. How will adoption and business outcomes be measured?

## Initial Assessment Recommendation

Based on the initial information, a Copilot Readiness Assessment appears appropriate.

The assessment should help the customer evaluate organizational readiness, identify valuable business use cases, review security and data considerations, and identify potential blockers before broader Copilot adoption.

Final scope should be confirmed with the customer, Microsoft stakeholders, and the assigned Inviso Technical Consultant.
