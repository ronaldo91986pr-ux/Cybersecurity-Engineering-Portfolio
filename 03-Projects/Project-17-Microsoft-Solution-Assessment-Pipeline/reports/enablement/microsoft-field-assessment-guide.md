# Microsoft Field — Solution Assessment Quick Guide

> Fictional enablement material created for portfolio and training purposes.

## Purpose

This guide provides a simple framework for identifying customers who may benefit from a Microsoft Solution Assessment.

The objective is not to perform the technical assessment, but to recognize customer needs, ask effective discovery questions, identify the appropriate solution area, and coordinate the next step with the assessment team.

---

# 1. Azure Infrastructure

## Listen For

Customers may say:

- "We have a lot of on-premises servers."
- "We're considering moving workloads to Azure."
- "Our infrastructure costs are increasing."
- "We don't know which applications can move to the cloud."
- "We're concerned about migration downtime."

## Discovery Questions

Ask:

1. What business objective is driving the cloud initiative?
2. What workloads are currently running on-premises?
3. Which applications are business-critical?
4. What concerns do you have about cost?
5. What downtime can the business tolerate?
6. Are there security or compliance requirements?
7. What is the expected migration timeline?

## Potential Assessment

**Infrastructure / Azure Migration Assessment**

The assessment can help the customer understand the existing environment, workload dependencies, migration readiness, potential risks, and possible next steps.

---

# 2. Microsoft Security

## Listen For

Customers may say:

- "We're seeing suspicious login attempts."
- "Not everyone uses MFA."
- "We don't know who has privileged access."
- "We need better visibility into security events."
- "We're concerned about identity or endpoint security."

## Discovery Questions

Ask:

1. How are identities currently managed?
2. Is MFA enforced?
3. How are privileged accounts protected?
4. How are permissions reviewed?
5. How are endpoints protected?
6. How are security alerts monitored?
7. Are there compliance requirements?

## Potential Assessment

**Microsoft Security Assessment**

The assessment can help identify potential gaps across identity, access, endpoints, cloud security, and security operations.

---

# 3. Copilot & AI

## Listen For

Customers may say:

- "We're interested in Microsoft 365 Copilot."
- "We want employees to use AI."
- "We're concerned about our data."
- "We don't know whether our environment is ready."
- "We need to demonstrate business value from Copilot."

## Discovery Questions

Ask:

1. What business problems should Copilot help solve?
2. Which users or departments would benefit first?
3. What Microsoft 365 services are currently used?
4. How are permissions managed?
5. How is sensitive information protected?
6. What are the organization's primary Copilot use cases?
7. How will adoption and business value be measured?

## Potential Assessment

**Copilot Readiness Assessment**

The assessment can help evaluate organizational readiness, identify business use cases, surface security and data considerations, and identify blockers before broader adoption.

---

# Qualification Framework

For every opportunity, identify:

**Business Need → Solution Area → Stakeholders → Current Environment → Risks → Blockers → Assessment Fit → Next Action**

Do not assume an assessment is appropriate simply because a technology is mentioned.

Discovery should determine whether the customer's objectives align with the assessment and whether the necessary stakeholders are prepared to proceed.

---

# Stakeholder Communication

Different stakeholders require different levels of information.

## Business Decision Maker

Focus on:

- Business outcomes
- Risk
- Cost
- Productivity
- Timeline
- Strategic value

## Microsoft Seller

Focus on:

- Customer objective
- Assessment opportunity
- Qualification status
- Blockers
- Next action

## Technical Consultant

Focus on:

- Current environment
- Technical requirements
- Known dependencies
- Security requirements
- Assessment scope
- Information required before kickoff

## Customer Technical Team

Focus on:

- Environment
- Requirements
- Dependencies
- Security
- Technical constraints
- Required information

---

# Key Principle

A successful Solution Assessment Specialist connects:

**Microsoft Field → Customer → Technical Consultant**

The goal is to maintain alignment, remove blockers, communicate assessment value, and move qualified opportunities toward successful kickoff.
