# Contoso Manufacturing — Opportunity Qualification

## Opportunity Overview

**Customer:** Contoso Manufacturing  
**Microsoft AE:** Alex Rivera  
**Solution Area:** Azure Infrastructure  
**Assessment:** Infrastructure Assessment  
**Current Stage:** Qualification  
**Priority:** High  

## Initial Customer Situation

Contoso Manufacturing has approximately 500 employees and operates 50 on-premises Windows servers.

The organization is considering moving workloads to Microsoft Azure but has concerns regarding:

- Migration cost
- Security
- Business downtime
- Workload suitability
- Migration planning

## Discovery Questions

### Business Objectives

1. What is driving the organization to consider Azure?
2. What business outcomes are you hoping to achieve?
3. Is there a specific timeline driving the migration?

### Current Environment

4. What workloads and applications are currently running on the 50 servers?
5. Which workloads are considered business-critical?
6. Are there dependencies between applications or servers?

### Security and Compliance

7. Are there specific security or regulatory requirements we need to consider?
8. What identity and access controls are currently being used?

### Migration Concerns

9. What are your primary concerns regarding downtime?
10. What are your biggest cost concerns?
11. Have you previously attempted or evaluated cloud migration?

## Initial Assessment Recommendation

Based on the information currently available, an Azure Infrastructure assessment appears appropriate.

The assessment should help provide visibility into the customer's existing environment and support informed discussions around workload suitability, migration planning, cost, security, and modernization opportunities.

Final assessment scope should be confirmed after customer discovery and alignment with the Microsoft and Inviso technical teams.
