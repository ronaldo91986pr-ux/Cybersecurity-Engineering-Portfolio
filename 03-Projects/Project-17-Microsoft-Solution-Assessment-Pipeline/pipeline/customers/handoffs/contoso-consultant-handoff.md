**Customer:** Contoso Manufacturing

**Solution Area:** Azure Infrastructure

---

## Business Objective

Contoso Manufacturing is evaluating Microsoft Azure as a potential platform for modernizing portions of its existing on-premises infrastructure.

The customer wants to understand whether Azure can provide improvements in scalability, security, operational efficiency, and infrastructure management.

---

## Current Environment

- Approximately 500 employees
- 50 on-premises Windows servers
- Multiple business workloads hosted locally
- Azure migration is under consideration
- Workload dependencies require further discovery

---

## Customer Concerns

### Cost
The customer wants better visibility into the potential financial impact of moving workloads to Azure.

### Security
The customer wants to understand how security, identity, access, and workload protection would be addressed in the cloud environment.

### Downtime
Business-critical applications must remain available, making migration planning and downtime reduction important considerations.

---

## Assessment Goals

The proposed assessment should help:

- Discover and understand the existing server environment
- Identify workload dependencies
- Evaluate workload suitability for Azure
- Identify potential migration risks
- Support cost and sizing discussions
- Identify security considerations
- Prioritize potential migration candidates
- Provide information for migration planning
