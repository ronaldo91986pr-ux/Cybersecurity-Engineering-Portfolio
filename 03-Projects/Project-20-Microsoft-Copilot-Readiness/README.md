# Project 20 — Microsoft 365 Copilot Readiness Assessment

## Project Overview

This project simulates the qualification and discovery process for a Microsoft 365 Copilot readiness opportunity.

The objective is to demonstrate how a Solution Assessment Specialist can identify business requirements, qualify the opportunity, uncover potential blockers, align stakeholders, and prepare an engagement for technical assessment.

This is a fictional portfolio scenario and does not represent a production customer assessment.

---

## Customer Scenario

**Customer:** Northwind Services

**Employees:** 2,500

**Environment:** Microsoft 365

**Business Goal:** Evaluate Microsoft 365 Copilot for an initial group of 300 employees.

The organization wants to improve employee productivity but needs to determine whether its business processes, information governance, security posture, and users are ready for Copilot adoption.

---

## Business Drivers

The customer identified several potential Copilot use cases:

- Summarizing meetings
- Drafting emails and documents
- Finding organizational information
- Summarizing lengthy documents
- Assisting employees with content creation
- Improving collaboration
- Reducing repetitive administrative work

The objective is not simply to deploy AI.

The organization needs to determine where Copilot can produce measurable business value.

## Discovery Questions

### Business

1. What business problems should Copilot help solve?
2. Which departments would receive the greatest value?
3. Which repetitive activities consume the most employee time?
4. How will the organization measure success?
5. Which users should participate in an initial deployment?

### Technology

1. Which Microsoft 365 services are currently used?
2. How are users and identities managed?
3. What licensing is currently available?
4. Are there technical dependencies that could affect adoption?

### Security & Data

1. How are permissions currently managed?
2. Who can access sensitive organizational information?
3. How is sensitive data classified and protected?
4. Are there overshared SharePoint or Teams resources?
5. How are privileged accounts protected?
6. Is MFA implemented?

### Adoption

1. Are employees familiar with generative AI?
2. What training will users require?
3. Who will own adoption?
4. How will feedback be collected?
5. How will successful use cases be expanded?

---

## Initial Findings

Discovery identified four areas requiring further evaluation.

### 1. Data Governance

The customer must understand how organizational information is classified, stored, shared, and protected.

### 2. Permissions

Existing permissions should be reviewed to identify unnecessary or excessive access.

### 3. Security

Identity and access controls should support secure Copilot adoption.

### 4. Adoption

Users require defined use cases, training, and measurable success criteria.

---

## Identified Blockers

| Blocker | Business Impact | Priority | Next Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Unclear data ownership | Potential inappropriate information access | High | Identify data owners |
| Excessive permissions | Increased information exposure risk | High | Review access model |
| Undefined success metrics | Difficult to demonstrate ROI | Medium | Define measurable KPIs |
| No adoption plan | Low user adoption risk | Medium | Develop enablement strategy |
